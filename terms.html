Scanned receipts are a starting point

Receipt scanning uses on-device text recognition and an AI model to turn a receipt into rows. It is very good, and it is not perfect: item names, prices, discounts and totals can be misread. Check the rows against the paper receipt before sharing a bill. You are responsible for the amounts you ask people to pay.
